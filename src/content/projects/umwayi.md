UMWAYI is the journal and hub this whole site is built around: a plain-markdown knowledge
system, not an app. One master file holds the current context across every project (career
prep, CMU, Routine Machine, the settlement engine), and daily journal entries update whichever
project they touch.

It's the thing that makes "the journal is the hub" a literal, checkable claim rather than a
metaphor. This site's own homepage pulls its open questions and open decisions straight from
UMWAYI's public repo at build time.
