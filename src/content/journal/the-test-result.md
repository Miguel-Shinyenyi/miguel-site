Picked database indexing, the thing I know best, deliberately, to give the test every chance
to confirm the fear: that I don't actually understand things, I just reproduce them.

The explanation came out complete. Problem, mechanism, tradeoff, in order, no gaps. Then,
immediately after finishing it, I predicted I'd already lost whoever was listening. That
prediction wasn't supported by anything in the explanation itself. It came after a correct
answer, not during a struggling one.

So the original hypothesis, a retrieval gap, doesn't hold, not for this. What's better
supported is narrower and more interesting: knowing the material was never the problem.
Trusting a finished answer to actually be finished, and reading a room well enough to know
how much of it to say, that's the real, untested skill. Different problem. Different fix.
